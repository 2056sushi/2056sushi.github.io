Research

EurIPS 2025 · Copenhagen

Interpretability as Alignment: Making Internal Understanding a Design Principle

Compares four leading approaches to AI safety and model oversight, evaluating how each performs in practice, and argues that mechanistic interpretability works best when built into a model's design rather than applied as an afterthought.

Accepted, EMNLP 2027 (NLLP Workshop)

What Do Compliance Detectors Read?

Introduces ICS, a lightweight scoring method that reads model behavior directly from internal activations in a single forward pass, avoiding the retraining or rule-based checks that auditing usually requires. Benchmarked across 20 domains with a 0.952 mean AUROC — matching or exceeding existing methods at a fraction of the cost.
